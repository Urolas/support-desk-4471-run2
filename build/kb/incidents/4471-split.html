  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Day-first tenants get the day and month swapped: booked 2 December, invite says 12 February.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first tenants on that form type month first, as the hint asks, and the form reads it day first. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not by a swap. 12 May becomes 11 May; Lisbon gets Tuesday and Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting; an invite arrives before its booking. The desk noted these reports as one-day moves and counts them under parser by tenant; the table keeps that count and gives them their own row too. A swap cannot move a date by one day. This looks like a time zone problem, not yet confirmed.

What intake guessed

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant. The tag is wrong for a large share of the hint reports. Do not triage on it.
